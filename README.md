Entry for the [gta6-clipper-01](https://make.fun/c/gta6-clipper-01) bounty on make.fun: a vertical GTA 6 clip cut for the most YouTube views.

## The clip

The clip opens on the Launch Trailer's biggest beat — Lucia firing a rifle with a fireball erupting behind her — then runs a fast montage of the trailer's best action: Jason and Lucia at sunset, the neon nightclub, a police drift down Ocean Drive, a motorcycle mid-air over the city, Jason with a rifle against the night skyline, the beach aerial, and the sunset embrace, before returning to the explosion. It closes with make.fun's pitch on screen: "Make clips like this with AI and earn money on make.fun" (make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01).

The video itself is the entry: the house reads it through the YouTube Data API and places go by its views. This repository only carries the video's address in `VIDEO.json`; `main.ts` copies it to the output directory for the gate.
